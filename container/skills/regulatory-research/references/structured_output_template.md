# Structured Regulatory Analysis Template

Use this template when conducting comprehensive regulatory research that requires detailed analysis of multiple provisions, cross-references, or complex compliance requirements. This format ensures all findings are organized, traceable, and verifiable.

**CRITICAL REQUIREMENTS:**
- **Every statement about laws/regulations MUST have inline citations** [Section citation]
- **Include your research thought process and reasoning** throughout
- **Explicitly identify research gaps** and sources you couldn't access
- **Provide verification recommendations** at the end

## Usage Guidelines

**When to use this template:**
- Query requires analysis of 3+ regulatory sections
- Multiple cross-references must be traced
- Compliance involves multiple steps or requirements
- Ambiguities or interpretation questions exist
- User needs a comprehensive, verifiable analysis

**When NOT to use this template:**
- Simple definitional queries with single-source answers
- Questions that can be answered conversationally with inline citations
- User has requested a specific different format

**CRITICAL: Always remember to include:**
- ✅ Inline citations after EVERY factual statement
- ✅ Explanation of your research thought process
- ✅ Identification of research gaps and limitations
- ✅ Verification recommendations
- ✅ Callout boxes for critical findings

**Customization:**
- Remove sections that are not applicable (but explain why)
- Add sections if needed for the specific analysis
- Adjust the level of detail based on query complexity
- Maintain the core structure to ensure traceability and transparency

**Presentation:**
For complex analyses, present this as a markdown artifact so the user has a standalone, downloadable document they can reference and verify.
